# VS CodexFolio risk register

Status: initial implementation risk baseline

Scales: likelihood and impact are Low, Medium, or High. The named owner is the accountable module or release function, not a person.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Early warning / trigger | Mitigation and required evidence | Owner | Residual risk |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|

## Risk review cadence

- Review this register at every milestone entry and exit.
- A new external data source, credential-bearing path, cross-workspace transfer, or background network call requires a threat-model update before implementation.
- Any High/Critical risk without named tests or an executable rollback blocks the affected stable milestone.
- Accepted residual risks must appear in release notes and the support matrix when user-visible.
