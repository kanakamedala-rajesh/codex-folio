# Separate companion lifetime from consent and attribution

Milestone 5A ([specification #82](https://github.com/kanakamedala-rajesh/codex-folio/issues/82), decisions 4–5, 10–16 and 19; contract ticket #83) separates having a usable companion from authorizing data collection. Plain startup starts or reuses the single per-user owner, keeps it usable from picker through foreground launch, and leaves it available after Codex exits. This extends [ADR 0016](0016-run-one-service-per-os-user.md)'s on-demand lifetime and narrows "persistent installation" to OS-login enrollment; it does not require enrollment for a companion to outlive a child.

This partially supersedes [ADR 0012](0012-make-background-collection-explicit-and-privacy-first.md)'s association of collection with an explicitly installed service. Offer background metadata collection once and remember the choice independently of service lifetime; refusal retains launching and on-demand refresh. [ADR 0023](0023-use-native-unprivileged-user-services.md)'s explicit, reversible native OS-login enrollment remains unchanged. Ordinary startup grants neither consent. Offer explicit companion stop and deferred stop while Managed Launches run, without terminating Codex; forced external owner termination must not be presented as uninterrupted tracking. Preserve the sole writer, transparent foreground streams/input/signals/status, and safe identity/state prerequisites.

[ADR 0030](0030-make-onboarding-resumable-and-fail-closed.md) remains fail-closed and resumable: existing homes can be registered as Referenced Identity Homes with an explanation that direct Codex shares them, while additional identities default to Managed Identity Homes. Valid authentication is reused; Codex owns login and refresh. Pending Profiles remain unselectable until validated. Terminal-assisted browser setup shows copyable instructions and waiting status immediately, detects actual operation completion without rerunning login, and exposes no raw authentication output. Registration does not consent to history import.

Supported history import requires a separate source/import choice and leaves source files intact. Unknown ownership is **Unassigned History**, not a selectable Identity Profile or an inference from today's login. Overall history may display it explicitly, but individual-profile totals and **Combined Identity View** (a profile aggregate) must exclude unassigned records. Source/session identity and provenance survive assignment, reassignment, and return to Unassigned; user assignment is distinct from evidence-established ownership. Support individual/bulk correction, consistent aggregate recalculation, and deduplication across imports and profile registration without merging unrelated sessions solely by timestamp. Migrate existing linked observations without inventing stronger attribution.

[ADRs 0014](0014-use-only-supported-read-only-data-sources.md), [0017](0017-store-only-allowlisted-normalized-metrics.md), and [0018](0018-keep-project-identity-app-local.md) remain binding. Preserve source, units, availability, freshness, and coverage; import only supported metrics actually present. Missing historical quota/credit snapshots stay unavailable rather than reconstructed or zero. New imported/assigned records obey existing retention, scoped purge, export, and project-path privacy boundaries; transcripts and other excluded content never become analytics. Review this contract before dependent implementation; it changes no application behavior in ticket #83.
